This Privacy Policy describes how Quieto ("the App") handles information when you use it on iOS or Android.

## Everything is generated on your device

Quieto plays relaxing sounds and noise to fall asleep to. Every sound — white noise, rain, ocean waves, a campfire and the rest of the catalogue — is synthesised live, on your device, from mathematical descriptions of sound. Nothing is recorded, nothing is a file, and there is no server: the App has no way to upload anything, because there is nothing resembling "your content" to upload in the first place.

## Notifications

While a sound is playing, the App shows a persistent notification with playback controls (play, pause, stop) so you can control it from your lock screen without unlocking your phone. This uses your device's standard media-notification permission and has nothing to do with push notifications: Quieto has no server to send one from, and no message is ever sent to your device remotely.

## What is stored on your device

Quieto stores your preferences locally, using the operating system's standard app storage: your language, whether scene animations and their sleep-timer dimming are turned on, and your last volume level. That is the full list. None of it ever leaves your device.

## No data collection

Quieto does not collect, transmit or share any personal information, and it makes no network requests of any kind — at no point, for any reason. Specifically:

- **No account or registration** — no name, email address or any other personal identifier is required or requested.
- **No analytics or tracking** — no analytics SDK, advertising network or third-party tracking service is used.
- **No location data** — the App does not access your device's location.
- **No camera or microphone** — the App does not access your camera or microphone.
- **No purchases** — Quieto is free, with no in-app purchases, no subscriptions and no ads, by design rather than as a launch promotion.

## Children's Privacy

Quieto does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under the age of 13.
