This Privacy Policy describes how ChessCounter ("the App") handles information when you use it on iOS or Android.

## Everything runs on your device

ChessCounter runs entirely on your device. Every chess lesson, the opponent you play against in Free play, and the board itself are all computed locally by the App, and work without an internet connection. ChessCounter has no server component of its own and sends none of your data anywhere.

The only time the App goes online is to talk to the App Store or Google Play about ChessCounter Pro, described below.

## What is stored on your device

ChessCounter stores three things locally, using the operating system's standard app storage:

- **Your preferences**, appearance (light/dark/auto), mute, language and piece art theme.
- **Your lesson progress**, which opening variants you have started or completed.
- **Your points and day streak**, the running totals shown on the app's home screen.
- **Whether ChessCounter Pro is unlocked**, so it keeps working without a connection.

None of this ever leaves your device, and none of it is shared with anyone, including the developer.

## ChessCounter Pro (in-app purchase)

ChessCounter Pro is an optional one-time purchase that unlocks every opening variant. It is sold through the App Store on iOS and Google Play on Android, and the purchase is handled entirely by Apple or Google under their own privacy policies. The developer never sees your name, payment details or store account.

To show the price and to check whether you own Pro, the App asks the store for the product and for your past purchases of it: when the App starts, when you open the purchase screen, when you buy, and when you tap "Restore purchases". The App only keeps the answer, whether Pro is unlocked, on your device.

## No data collection

ChessCounter does not collect, transmit or share any personal information. Specifically:

- **No account or registration**, no name, email address or any other personal identifier is required or requested.
- **No analytics or tracking**, no analytics SDK, advertising network or third-party tracking service is used.
- **No location data**, the App does not access your device's location.
- **No camera or microphone**, the App does not access your camera, microphone or contacts.
- **No subscriptions**, ChessCounter Pro is a one-time purchase, and no purchase information is collected by the developer.

## Children's Privacy

ChessCounter does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under the age of 13.
