This Privacy Policy describes how tanis.codes handles data, cookies and advertising.

## Who is responsible

This site is run by Estanislao Pérez Nartallo. For any question about this policy, you can reach out through any of the profiles linked in the site footer.

## A static blog

tanis.codes is a static blog. There are no user accounts, no forms and no comments system, so no personal data is collected directly through the site.

## Google AdSense

This site shows ads served by Google AdSense. Google and its advertising partners use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites. You can learn more about how Google uses this data at [policies.google.com/technologies/ads](https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads), and control ad personalization at [google.com/settings/ads](https://www.google.com/settings/ads).

Visitors from the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland are shown a consent message managed by Google before ads are personalized. You can change your choice at any time through that same consent message.

## Cloudflare Web Analytics

This site uses Cloudflare Web Analytics to measure aggregate traffic. It does not use cookies, does not perform fingerprinting and does not track visitors across sites. More information is available at [cloudflare.com/web-analytics](https://www.cloudflare.com/web-analytics/).

## No Google Analytics

This site does not use Google Analytics.

## Controlling cookies

You can view, block or delete cookies through your browser's settings. How to do this depends on the browser you use; check your browser's documentation for instructions.

## Outbound links

This site links to third-party websites. tanis.codes is not responsible for the content or privacy practices of those sites.
